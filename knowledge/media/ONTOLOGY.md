# Media Domain Ontology & Concept Map

## Key Entities & Frameworks

### 1. Content Operations & Publishing
- **Blog Management**: Content calendars, draft stages, tag taxonomies, SEO metadata.
- **Transcript Pipeline**: Speech-to-text intake, cleaning, entity extraction, and synthesis.
- **Distribution Channels**: Long-form publishing, newsletters, social syndication pipelines.

### 2. Strategic Communication
- **Audience Positioning**: Value proposition alignment, tone adjustment per channel.
- **Content Reuse**: Turning primary long-form knowledge assets into bite-sized summaries.

## Inter-Domain Relationships
- **Media -> Arts**: Content aesthetics, typography, and visual assets are defined in `knowledge/arts/`.
- **Media -> Business**: Messaging directly supports monetization, brand strategy, and product positioning (`knowledge/business/`).
- **Media -> Technology**: Publishing relies on CMS infrastructure, static site generators, and RSS/API distribution (`knowledge/technology/`).
