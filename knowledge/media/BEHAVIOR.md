# Media Domain Behavioral Guidelines

## Editorial & Production Rules
1. **Source Authenticity**: Transcripts and raw media exports stored in `raw/` must remain unedited to preserve original intent and phrasing.
2. **Editorial Consistency**: Content guidelines, tone-of-voice directives, and distribution playbooks in `synthesized/` must align with the target audience and channel constraints.
3. **SEO & Publishing Integrity**: All blog posts and public media assets must maintain validated frontmatter tags, structured metadata, and clean URL slugs.

## Synthesis & Processing Rules
- Summarize long-form video or audio transcripts into structured articles and social snippets inside `synthesized/`.
- Coordinate visual branding rules with `knowledge/arts/`.
