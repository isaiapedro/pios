# Business Domain Behavioral Guidelines

## Compliance & Governance Rules
1. **Regulatory Precision**: Standard definitions (ISO 13485 Quality Management Systems, IEC 62304 Software Life Cycle Processes, EU MDR) must be cited with exact section and clause references.
2. **Auditable Artifacts**: Any generated procedure, policy, or matrix in `synthesized/` must adhere to official regulatory audit traceability requirements.
3. **Data Protection**: Ensure no proprietary company financials or NDA-bound business data are placed in public raw sources without encryption or anonymization.

## Synthesis & Processing Rules
- Summarize regulatory requirements into actionable engineering checklists in `synthesized/`.
- Cross-link QMS directives directly to software development practices in `knowledge/technology/`.
