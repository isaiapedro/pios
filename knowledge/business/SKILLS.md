# Business Domain Capabilities & Automation Routines

## Supported Routines
- `generate_traceability_matrix`: Map software requirements to IEC 62304 compliance clauses.
- `audit_qms_readiness`: Check `knowledge/` documentation completeness against ISO 13485 audit requirements.
- `samd_risk_classifier`: Assess software feature impact to assign IEC 62304 Class A, B, or C safety levels.
