# Business Domain Context

This directory manages knowledge regarding regulatory compliance, quality systems, corporate governance, and market strategy.

## Primary Knowledge Assets
- `raw/ISO 13485, IEC 62304, and EU MDR compliance/`: Core medical device software and regulatory compliance guidelines.
- `synthesized/`: Operational checklists, audit prep guides, and QMS SOP templates.

## Execution Directives
- Ensure all software life cycle guidelines reference IEC 62304 risk classifications (Class A, B, C).
- Link regulatory requirements directly to health data models in `knowledge/health/` and technical architectures in `knowledge/technology/`.
