# Business Domain Ontology & Concept Map

## Key Entities & Frameworks

### 1. Regulatory & Quality Management (QMS)
- **ISO 13485**: Quality management system requirements for medical devices.
- **IEC 62304**: Software life cycle processes (Design, Architecture, Testing, Risk Management).
- **EU MDR (2017/745)**: European Union Medical Device Regulation compliance and technical documentation.

### 2. Operational Strategy
- **Risk Management (ISO 14971)**: Risk analysis, evaluation, and mitigation controls.
- **Verification & Validation (V&V)**: Traceability from user requirements to software tests.

## Inter-Domain Relationships
- **Business -> Technology**: IEC 62304 requires version-controlled repositories, documented software architecture (`knowledge/technology/`), and automated test execution.
- **Business -> Health**: Regulatory scopes determine allowed clinical workflows and standard data handling (`knowledge/health/`).
