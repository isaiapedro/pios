# Knowledge Domain Directives

This directory houses the central knowledge graph organized by objective domains.

## Key Subdirectories
- `health/`: Health data standards, medical informatics, and regulatory specs.
- `business/`: Compliance, operational frameworks, and market strategy.
- `technology/`: Architecture, engineering, and dev protocols.
- `media/`: Content strategy, editorial workflows, and media operations.
- `arts/`: Design systems, creative writing, and aesthetic frameworks.

## Operational Rules
- Check `ONTOLOGY.md` before creating new cross-domain relationships.
- Follow `MIGRATION.md` when reorganizing legacy folders or ingest paths.
- Ensure all synthesized files maintain standard YAML frontmatter headers.
